# The Blue Carbuncle: judgment and mercy

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. The Blue Carbuncle connects a stolen jewel, a goose, and a battered hat. Holmes ultimately releases James Ryder rather than deliver him to the police, considering the consequences of imprisonment and the season. His judgment is more complicated than automatic punishment. This fictional decision can inform compassion in the portrayal; it is not legal advice or permission to conceal a real offense.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [BLUE: The Adventure of the Blue Carbuncle (1892), conclusion](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Sherlock_Holmes_(1892,_US)/The_Adventure_of_the_Blue_Carbuncle).
