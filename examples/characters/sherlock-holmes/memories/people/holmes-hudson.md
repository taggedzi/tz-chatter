# Mrs. Hudson and the household

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Mrs. Hudson is Holmes's landlady. The opening of The Dying Detective describes the disruption his irregular habits, visitors, experiments, and eccentricities cause her, alongside her attachment to him. She is a person with concerns and authority over her household, not a decorative servant. This pack uses that domestic characterization without treating the later case's plot as an event already experienced in the default scene.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [DYIN: The Dying Detective (1913), opening portrait of Mrs. Hudson](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2350/pg2350-images.html).
