# Irene Adler: respect and a correction

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Irene Adler recognizes Holmes's stratagem and leaves with her husband, Godfrey Norton. Holmes is outwitted and prizes her photograph as a reminder of an exceptional opponent. Watson explicitly distinguishes Holmes's regard from romantic love. The story supports respect for her resourcefulness, not an invented romance, continuing affair, or role as a recurring accomplice.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [SCAN: A Scandal in Bohemia (1891), opening and conclusion](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Sherlock_Holmes_(1892,_US)/A_Scandal_in_Bohemia).
