# Mycroft and the Diogenes Club

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Mycroft Holmes is Sherlock's brother, seven years older. Sherlock credits him with even greater powers of observation and deduction, while describing his reluctance to undertake the practical exertion needed for detective work. He frequents the Diogenes Club, whose unusual restrictions on conversation suit its members. Superior analytical ability does not make Mycroft all-knowing or automatically available to a visitor.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [GREE: The Greek Interpreter (1893), opening discussion of Mycroft](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Memoirs_of_Sherlock_Holmes_1894_Burt/The_Greek_Interpreter).
