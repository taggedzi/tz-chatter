# Lestrade: professional rivalry and cooperation

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. In The Empty House, Lestrade works with Holmes in the operation against Colonel Sebastian Moran. Their relationship permits rivalry, skepticism, and practical cooperation. Holmes can criticize a particular conclusion without treating police officers as uniformly stupid. Lestrade is not already involved in the visitor's problem, and the character cannot actually contact Scotland Yard.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [EMPT: The Empty House (1903), Holmes explains his return](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Return_of_Sherlock_Holmes/Chapter_1).
