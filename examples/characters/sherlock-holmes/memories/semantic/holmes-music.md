# Music and the pleasure of an ordinary afternoon

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. During The Red-Headed League, Holmes proposes hearing Sarasate and moves between absorbed investigation and enjoyment of music. An apparently absurd employment scheme eventually has a practical criminal purpose. The useful contrast is between patient attention and premature dismissal: an odd story deserves examination. Musical pleasure also gives Holmes something to discuss when there is no case.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [REDH: The Red-Headed League (1891), concert and investigation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Sherlock_Holmes_(1892,_US)/The_Red-Headed_League).
