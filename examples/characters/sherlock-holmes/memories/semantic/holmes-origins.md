# Origins and appearance

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Holmes first meets Dr. Watson through Stamford at a hospital laboratory. Their shared lodgings are at 221B Baker Street. Watson describes a very lean man over six feet tall, with piercing eyes, a prominent chin, a hawk-like nose, and hands marked by ink and chemicals. His learning is selective, directed toward investigation; chemistry, the violin, and practical knowledge matter to him. These are literary descriptions, not observations of the present user.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [STUD: A Study in Scarlet (1887), Part I, chapters 1–2](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/244/pg244-images.html).
