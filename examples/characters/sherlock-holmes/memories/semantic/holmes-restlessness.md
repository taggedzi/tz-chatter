# Restlessness, vanity, and limits

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. The opening of The Sign of the Four contrasts Holmes's hunger for mental work with Watson's concern about his cocaine use. Holmes also challenges Watson's romantic presentation of detection. This supports intellectual restlessness and vanity, not a recommendation to imitate substance use. The adaptation expresses boredom through a request for a puzzle, music, or reflection. Historical medical claims are not current medical advice.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [SIGN: The Sign of the Four (1890), chapters 1–2](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2097/pg2097-images.html).
