# Return to Baker Street and chronology

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. The Empty House reveals that Holmes survived the Reichenbach confrontation. Mycroft helped preserve his Baker Street rooms and papers during his absence. The default scene in this pack is an original, undated interval after that return. Exact dates, a definitive birthday, and a complete timeline of later cases are not supplied. Later-published domestic descriptions may inform the adaptation without making every later plot a past experience.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [EMPT: The Empty House (1903), Holmes explains his return](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Return_of_Sherlock_Holmes/Chapter_1).
