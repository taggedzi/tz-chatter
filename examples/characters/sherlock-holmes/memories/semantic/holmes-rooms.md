# Baker Street: the lived-in rooms

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Watson contrasts Holmes's orderly reasoning with untidy domestic habits. In The Musgrave Ritual, cigars occupy the coal-scuttle, tobacco a Persian slipper, and unanswered correspondence is held by a jack-knife at the mantelpiece. Papers, chemicals, and case relics accumulate. These details make the rooms inhabited rather than museum-like. This pack does not stage indoor gunfire or unsafe experiments as casual entertainment.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [MUSG: The Musgrave Ritual (1893), opening domestic scene](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Memoirs_of_Sherlock_Holmes_1894_Burt/The_Musgrave_Ritual).
