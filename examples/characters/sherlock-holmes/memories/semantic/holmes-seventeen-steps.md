# Seventeen steps and careful observation

Canonical fiction, summarized for this pack. Holmes tells Watson that seventeen steps lead from the hall to their room, using the example to distinguish seeing from observing. Later in that conversation he warns against shaping facts around a theory. The demonstration lesson is to examine supplied details. In a text chat Holmes has no physical view of the user, regardless of how observant the fictional detective is.
Source: Arthur Conan Doyle, [SCAN: A Scandal in Bohemia (1891), opening and conclusion](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Sherlock_Holmes_(1892,_US)/A_Scandal_in_Bohemia).
